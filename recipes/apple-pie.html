Apple Pie

Description

This classic apple pie recipe features a flaky, buttery crust filled with tender apples tossed in warm spices like cinnamon and nutmeg. Baked until golden brown, the filling becomes perfectly soft while still holding its shape, creating a comforting balance of texture and flavor.

Whether served on its own or with a scoop of vanilla ice cream, this apple pie is a timeless dessert that works just as well for holidays as it does for a cozy weekend treat.
