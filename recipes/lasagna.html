Lasagna

Description

This classic lasagna recipe layers tender pasta sheets with a rich meat sauce, creamy ricotta, and melted cheese for a hearty and satisfying dish.

Baked until bubbling and golden on top, the layers come together to create a comforting meal that's perfect for family dinners or special occasions.

Ingredients

  • Lasagna Noodles
  • Ground Beef
  • Tomato Sauce
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Ricotta Cheese
  • Mozzarella Cheese
  • Parmesan Cheese
  • Olive Oil
  • Salt and Black Pepper

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F.
  2. Cook the lasagna noodles according to the package instructions, then drain and set aside.
  3. Heat olive oil in a pan and cook the ground beef with chopped onion and garlic until browned.
  4. Add the tomato sauce, season with salt and pepper, and simmer for several minutes.
  5. Layer noodles, meat sauce, and cheeses in a baking dish, repeating until all ingredients are used.
  6. Top with mozzarella and Parmesan cheese.
  7. Bake for 35-40 minutes, until the cheese is melted and lightly browned.
  8. Let rest for a few minutes before slicing and serving.
