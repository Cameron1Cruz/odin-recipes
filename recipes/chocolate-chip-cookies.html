Chocolate Chip Cookies

Description

These classic chocolate chip cookies are soft in the center with lightly crisp edges and filled with rich chocolate chips throughout.

Simple to make and baked until just golden, they're a timeless treat that works for everything from casual snacks to sharing with friends and family.

Ingredients

  • All-Purpose Flour
  • Baking Soda
  • Salt
  • Unsalted Butter
  • Brown Sugar
  • Granulated Sugar
  • Eggs
  • Vanilla Extract
  • Chocolate Chips

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
  2. In a bowl, mix the flour, baking soda, and salt.
  3. In a separate bowl, cream together the butter, brown sugar, and granulated sugar.
  4. Beat in the eggs and vanilla extract.
  5. Gradually add the dry ingredients to the wet ingredients and mix until combined.
  6. Stir in the chocolate chips.
  7. Scoop dough onto a baking sheet, spacing cookies evenly.
  8. Bake for 10-12 minutes, until the edges are lightly golden.
  9. Let cool briefly before transferring to a wire rack.
